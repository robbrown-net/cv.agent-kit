Companion to `masters/operations-leader.md`. The master is three pages. Cutting in this order,
top first, takes it to two pages at 10.5pt Arial. Items 1 to 6 are usually enough.

## Summary variants

**Variant A, scale-up and growth emphasis.** Fifteen years building operations that scale: merchant
onboarding cut from 21 days to 6, GBP 3.2m of annual run cost removed through automation, and an
operations function grown into 62 people with one management rhythm. I work in fintech, banking
and retail, and I start every engagement by measuring how the work actually flows before I change
anything. I put regulatory control inside the process so audits stay routine, with zero major FCA
safeguarding findings at the last review. I build the team leaders who carry the change on, and I
report to the executive team in numbers they can act on.

**Variant B, transformation and change emphasis.** Fifteen years leading operational change in
banking, retail and payments, from a GBP 4.1m Lean programme at Northwind Bank to a click-and-collect
launch across 300 stores that generated GBP 18m in its first year. I redesign the workflow, the
organisation and the management routines together, because a new process fails when the team around
it does not change. At Example Payments I cleared a backlog of 4,800 chargebacks to 300 in 12 weeks
and cut onboarding from 21 days to 6. I bring regulated delivery discipline and a practical
approach to AI-assisted case handling, with human review built in.

## Known thin spots

- **Direct P&L ownership.** The bank shows cost savings and run cost removed, not revenue targets
  or a P&L held. If a JD asks for P&L accountability, ask Alex before claiming it.
- **Outsourcing and vendor management.** No achievement covers a supplier contract or offshore
  operation. Do not imply one.
- **Customer-facing contact centre leadership.** `examplepay-06` is a pilot, not a full service
  redesign. Describe it as a pilot until gaps.md item 5 is answered.
- **Team size after 2025.** See gaps.md item 2 before using any number other than 62.
