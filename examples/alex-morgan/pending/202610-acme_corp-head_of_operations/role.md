# Head of Operations, Acme Corp

**Company:** Acme Corp, a UK payments and retail technology company serving around 2,000 merchants
**Location:** London, hybrid (three days a week in the office)
**Salary:** not stated
**Closing date:** 30 October 2026

## About the role

Acme Corp is growing quickly and its operations have not kept pace. The Head of Operations will
own merchant onboarding, settlement, disputes and customer support, and will report to the Chief
Operating Officer. This is a hands-on leadership role: you will redesign how work flows, build
the management routines, and show the executive team where the cost and risk sit.

## Requirements

- Proven experience leading an operations function of 50 or more people
- A track record of reducing cost to serve and shortening customer cycle times
- Experience working in an FCA regulated environment, including audits and control evidence
- Process improvement skills such as Lean or Six Sigma, with measured results
- Practical experience of automation or AI-assisted operations
- Strong stakeholder management with the executive team
- Experience of coaching and developing team leaders

## Nice to have

- Retail or ecommerce operations background
- Experience of a scale-up or high growth environment
